Yangon Early Warning System

Yangon Township Early Warning

Choose your township to see disease warnings, risk levels and what you can do to stay safe. This is a school project (CATALYST) about epidemic awareness in Myanmar.

Please read: This is an educational page. It is not an official disease surveillance system and it is not a live feed. Every warning here comes from a real report, and each one shows its source link and date. Health authorities in Myanmar publish few township numbers, so we show only what was published and say when a number is missing. Always follow instructions from the Ministry of Health and your local health workers.
