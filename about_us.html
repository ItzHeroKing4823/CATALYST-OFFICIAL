Building a More Prepared Community. Together.

CATALYST is a student-led awareness project that aims to educate Myanmar communities about epidemics. We believe that knowledge, awareness, and preparation can help people make safer decisions during public health emergencies.

Prevention

Prevention and Safety

Prevention is an important part of reducing disease transmission. Our website shares simple information about hygiene, sanitation, mosquito control, vaccination, and responsible health practices.

  • Practise good hand hygiene.
  • Remove standing water to reduce mosquito breeding.
  • Follow reliable health advice.

Myth vs Fact

Understanding Myth vs Fact

False information can cause confusion during disease outbreaks. Our Myth vs Fact section helps visitors compare common health claims with information from reliable health organisations.

Example: Dengue Prevention

Myth: Herbal drinks can completely cure dengue fever.

Fact: There is no proven herbal cure for dengue. People with suspected dengue should seek medical advice.

Disease Information

Diseases We Explore

Examples of infectious diseases included in our educational project.

Disease How It Spreads General Prevention
Dengue Fever Through bites from infected mosquitoes. Remove standing water and prevent mosquito breeding.
Malaria Through bites from infected Anopheles mosquitoes. Use mosquito protection and follow health advice.
Tuberculosis Through the air when a person with infectious TB coughs or speaks. Early diagnosis, treatment, and good ventilation.
Measles Through the air and respiratory particles. Vaccination and avoiding exposure to infection.
Cholera Through contaminated food or water. Safe drinking water, sanitation, and handwashing.
Influenza Through respiratory particles and close contact. Hand hygiene, vaccination, and respiratory etiquette.

This table provides educational based information. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace professional medical advice.

Our Project Process

How We Develop CATALYST

Learn about the steps we take to create our epidemic awareness project.

We research epidemics and pandemics, their causes, transmission, effects, and prevention methods. We use reliable sources such as the World Health Organization to support our educational content.

We use our Epidemic Awareness and Preparedness survey to understand people's knowledge, concerns, and preparedness for future outbreaks. The responses help us identify areas that need more public awareness.

We develop our website to organise disease information, prevention tips, and awareness resources in one place. Our goal is to make the information clear and easy to explore.

We create communication materials, including awareness messages and a trailer, to encourage students, teachers, and communities to learn more about pandemic risks and preparedness.

Be Aware. Be Prepared. Be the Difference.

Through our ICT PBL project, we aim to develop an interactive website that uses creative and informative communication to raise awareness of major epidemic risks in Yangon. By providing reliable health information, emergency preparedness guidance, and township-based early warning updates, we hope to help communities understand potential threats, take preventive action, and reduce the impact of future outbreaks.
