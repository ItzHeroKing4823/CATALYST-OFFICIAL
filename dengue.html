Mosquito-borne virus

Dengue Fever (DENV 1-4)

Dengue is a viral disease spread by female Aedes mosquitoes. Severe cases cause plasma leakage, bleeding and shock. It is the disease most closely tied to the Myanmar rainy season.

Vector: Aedes aegypti and albopictus4 virus typesRisk: plasma leakage and shock
AedesMosquito that spreads dengueBites in the daytime
4-10 daysIncubation periodFrom bite to fever
Myanmar860+ cases, 4 deathsMyanmar, 1 Jan to 10 May 2025Ministry of Health. Lower than the same period of 2024.
Rainy-season alert June to October

Rain fills water drums, vases and old tyres in Yangon, Bago, Ayeyarwady and other towns, and mosquitoes breed in them.

01. Background

Origins and why a second infection is worse

Dengue is also called breakbone fever because of the deep joint and muscle pain it can cause.

Infection with one of the four virus types gives lifelong protection against that type only. A later infection with a different type can be more severe because old antibodies help the virus enter cells.

Vector facts

A daytime biter

Unlike the malaria mosquito, Aedes bites during the day and lives in and around homes.

02. Transmission

How dengue spreads in a community

Main route

1. Daytime mosquito bites

A mosquito bites an infected person, and about 8 to 12 days later it can pass the virus on with every bite.

Breeding sites

2. Standing water at home

Water storage drums, flower vases, discarded tyres and rain-filled rubbish hold the eggs and larvae.

Rare route

3. Blood and mother to baby

Spread through blood transfusion, organ transplant or from mother to baby at birth is rare.

03. Symptoms

Symptoms and warning signs

High fever

Sudden fever up to 40 C with a flushed face and red eyes.

Pain behind the eyes

Aching behind the eyeballs that is worse when the eyes move.

Muscle and joint pain

Deep aches that cause heavy tiredness.

Rash

A rash 3 to 4 days after the fever starts, sometimes with tiny skin bleeds.

Warning signs (usually days 3 to 7)

When the fever drops, the next 24 to 48 hours can be the most dangerous. Go to hospital at once for:

  • Strong, continuous stomach pain
  • Vomiting 3 or more times in 24 hours
  • Bleeding gums, nosebleeds, or blood in vomit or stool
  • Fast breathing, cold skin or extreme sleepiness

In Yangon call 192 for an ambulance. Read the emergency protocol.

04. Myanmar Context

Dengue in Myanmar

The Ministry of Health reported more than 860 dengue cases and 4 deaths between 1 January and 10 May 2025, lower than the same period of 2024. Cases normally climb as the rains begin, so April to May is the time to clean up breeding sites before the peak.

Children are often the most affected group. Do not give aspirin or ibuprofen for dengue fever. Use paracetamol and see a doctor.

Typical public health response: Community checks for larvae, larvicide in water storage tanks and fogging in wards with active cases.

Household prevention

  • Empty and scrub water storage drums every week.
  • Cover water tanks tightly and throw away old tyres and tins.
  • Use repellent with DEET or icaridin in the morning and afternoon.
