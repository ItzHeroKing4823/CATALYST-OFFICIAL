1. Overview

CATALYST is an educational project that shares information about infectious diseases and helps people in Yangon find hospitals. This page explains in simple words what information the website handles and where it goes.

The most important point: CATALYST has no server and no database. Information you type into the website stays in your own web browser on your own device.

2. Information the website handles

  • Account details (name, email, township and password) that you type in the Sign Up window. They are saved in your browser’s local storage so that you can sign in again on the same device.
  • Booked appointments on the Book Care page (name, date of birth, phone number, symptoms, appointment date and time). They are also saved in your browser only.
  • Symptom Checker answers are used to show your result on the screen. They are not saved and not sent anywhere.
  • Contact and newsletter forms are not connected to a sending service yet, so the message you type is not delivered or stored.

3. Other companies that load content

To show fonts, animations, pictures and directions, your browser loads files from other companies. When this happens, they can see your IP address and basic browser information, as for any website. These include:

  • Google Fonts (text fonts)
  • jsDelivr, unpkg and cdnjs (Bootstrap and AOS animations)
  • Unsplash and WHO (some pictures)
  • Google Maps (opens only when you press “Get Directions”)

Please read the privacy notices of these companies if you want to know how they handle information.

4. Cookies and local storage

CATALYST does not set tracking or advertising cookies. It uses your browser’s local storage for the account and appointment features described above. Companies listed in section 3 may use their own cookies or logs.

5. Your choices

  • Press Log Out at any time to end your session on the website.
  • On the My Account page you can edit your details or choose Delete account to remove your account from this browser.
  • You can remove everything the website has saved by clearing the site data for this website in your browser settings.

6. Security

Information in local storage is not encrypted. Anyone who can use your browser profile may be able to read it. Because this is a school project, please do not use a password you use anywhere else, and do not type sensitive medical details that you would not want others to see on a shared computer.

7. Medical information

CATALYST gives general health information and is not a medical service. Nothing on the website is a diagnosis or a replacement for a doctor. In an emergency, read the Emergency Protocol and call 192.

8. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the website changes. The date at the top shows the latest version. If you have a question, please use the Contact page.
