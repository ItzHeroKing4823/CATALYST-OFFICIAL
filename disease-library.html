10 Core Diseases

Disease Library

Ten infectious diseases, each with the latest Myanmar figures, how the infection spreads, the warning signs, and how a family can prevent it.

Showing 10 of 10 diseases.

Vector-Borne

Dengue Fever

A viral infection spread by Aedes mosquitoes that bite during the day. Cases rise in Myanmar's rainy season.

Read profile
Vector-Borne

Malaria

A parasite infection spread by the female Anopheles mosquito at night. Cases are rising in Myanmar.

Read profile
Airborne

Tuberculosis (TB)

A bacterial infection of the lungs spread through the air when a patient coughs. Myanmar has about 257,000 cases a year.

Read profile
Airborne

Measles

A very contagious viral infection with fever and a red rash. Myanmar's vaccine was 71% in 2025, so gaps remain.

Read profile
Airborne

Meningococcal Meningitis

A bacterial infection of the lining around the brain. A stiff neck and high fever mean the patient needs hospital care at once.

Read profile
Airborne

Avian Flu (H5N1)

A bird virus that can infect people who touch sick poultry. Myanmar had its first human case in 2007.

Read profile
Waterborne

Cholera

A bacterial infection from unsafe water or food that causes a severe watery diarrhoea. An active outbreak through Yangon, August 2026.

Read profile
Vector-Borne

Yellow Fever

A mosquito-borne virus that damages the liver and turns the skin yellow. Not found in Myanmar, but travellers need a vaccine certificate.

Read profile
Bloodborne

HIV and AIDS

A virus that weakens the immune system. Modern treatment keeps the virus low. Over 200,000 people in Myanmar are on treatment.

Read profile
Bloodborne

Ebola and Marburg

Rare filoviruses spread through body fluids. No case has been reported in Myanmar. Imported risk comes from travel.
