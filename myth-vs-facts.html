Clinical Misconceptions vs. Verified Facts Matrix

Disease / Category Spoken Rumor / Myth Verified Clinical Fact Authoritative Source
Dengue Virus MYTH

Raw papaya leaf juice can cure Dengue and rapidly restore falling blood platelets without hospital IV fluids.

FACT

No herbal cure destroys Dengue RNA. Clinical management requires strict intravenous fluid balance and platelet monitoring in hospitals.

WHO & MoH Myanmar
Influenza / Flu MYTH

Buying and taking strong over-the-counter antibiotics immediately cures acute viral flu and fevers.

FACT

Antibiotics target bacteria exclusively. Misusing them for viral influenza fuels global Antimicrobial Resistance (AMR).

National Health Lab
Tuberculosis MYTH

Tuberculosis (TB) is a hereditary family curse passed down through bloodlines.

FACT

TB is a contagious airborne bacterial infection transmitted via droplets. It is 100% curable with standard DOTS courses.

National TB Program
Malaria MYTH

Surviving Malaria once in forest zones gives you permanent lifetime immunity.

FACT

Immunity wanes rapidly. Multiple parasite species exist; bed nets and diagnostic testing remain critical.

Vector Borne Control
Typhoid MYTH

Typhoid fever is caused simply by eating spicy street food or working under the sun.

FACT

Typhoid results strictly from Salmonella Typhi ingested via contaminated water or fecal matter.

Environmental Health
Vaccines MYTH

Modern vaccines alter your personal cellular DNA permanently.

FACT

mRNA and vector components never enter the cell nucleus where DNA resides; they decompose within hours.

Public Verification Protocol

4 Steps to Verify Online Health Claims

Protect your household from dangerous viral medical rumors by evaluating information with this structured 4-step framework.

01
Institutional Source

Check Source Authority

Confirm whether claims originate from accredited bodies (WHO Myanmar, Ministry of Health and Sports) or peer-reviewed journals, rather than unverified social posts.

Verify Authority
02
Language Audit

Spot "Miracle" Claims

Exercise caution with posts promising "100% secret home cures" or instant remedies for complex viral infections.

Detect Sensationalism
03
Evidence Match

Cross-Reference Data

Verify recommendations across multiple independent clinical institutions before trying untested remedies or stopping prescribed treatments.

Cross-Check Facts
04
Medical Advice

Consult Registered Doctors

When experiencing persistent fever or respiratory illness, seek immediate consultation at a township hospital or verified healthcare facility.
