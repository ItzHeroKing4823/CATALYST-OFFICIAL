01. Myanmar outbreak and health reports

Reports about Myanmar used for the early warning page, disease pages and home page alerts.

  • Myanmar Ministry of Health (MoHS)

    Official announcements and public health notices.

    Used for: Where to find official updates.
  • WHO Myanmar country page

    WHO country overview and publications.

    Used for: Official updates link on the early warning page.
  • Bloomberg via The Star: cholera outbreak, 18 August 2026

    Yangon: about 500 hospitalised, 149 cholera positive, one death. Sittwe: 22 cholera cases, two deaths. Rakhine and Mon: 82 diarrhoea cases.

    Used for: Cholera page, early warning page, home alerts.
  • WHO Myanmar acute watery diarrhoea and cholera situation report, October 2024

    Yangon: 576 hospitalised cases. Rakhine: 2,470 diarrhoea cases.

    Used for: Cholera page.
  • UN Myanmar Health Cluster Bulletin, April 2026

    Malaria above the alert threshold in Tanintharyi and Southern Chin; measles risk in Rakhine and Chin; diarrhoea outbreak in Myitkyina camps.

    Used for: Yangon-wide facts and the malaria, measles and cholera pages.
  • UN Myanmar Health Cluster Bulletin, December 2025

    Further expansion of malaria cases and shortages of bed nets and supplies.

    Used for: Malaria page and national picture.
  • WHO Myanmar EPI Factsheet 2026

    Reported measles, diphtheria and polio cases 2019 to 2025; vaccine coverage 2019 to 2025.

    Used for: Measles page and national picture.
  • The Star (Bernama-Xinhua): Myanmar dengue, 23 May 2025

    More than 860 dengue cases and 4 deaths from 1 January to 10 May 2025, citing the Ministry of Health.

    Used for: Dengue page and national picture.
  • WHO tuberculosis profile for Myanmar (2022 data)

    TB incidence, deaths, drug-resistant TB, treatment coverage and TB/HIV.

    Used for: Tuberculosis page and national picture.
  • UNAIDS Myanmar country report 2024

    Treatment maintained for 220,000 clients; viral suppression; PrEP and methadone services.

    Used for: HIV page and national picture.
  • WHO Disease Outbreak News, 14 December 2007: Myanmar H5N1

    First confirmed human H5N1 case in Myanmar (Shan State).

    Used for: Avian flu page.
  • AsiaNews: Kayin State malaria, April 2023

    Report of malaria cases in Kayin rising about ten-fold since 2020.

    Used for: Malaria page. Older, news report.

03. About the data

Not a live feed. The early warning page and disease pages show what had been published when we reviewed them. Outbreaks change quickly, so use the Ministry of Health and WHO for the latest figures.

Reporting limits. Conflict, displacement and damaged health services mean many cases in Myanmar are not counted. Low reported numbers do not always mean few cases.

Hospital directory. The Yangon Hospital Directory lists 28 hospitals in 16 townships. Please call a hospital before you travel to check services and opening hours.

Not medical advice. CATALYST is a learning tool. A doctor must diagnose and treat illness.

Pictures. Photographs on the site are stored in the project’s image folder. Fonts come from Google Fonts (Inter), and the early warning page is drawn by the site itself.
